Report the shape of the knowledge graph. Read-only.

1. Count notes and links.
2. Find hubs (notes with an unusually high link count) and bridges (notes that connect
   otherwise separate clusters).
3. Find clusters and name what each is about.
4. List orphan notes (no inbound and no outbound links).

Return a short report. Do not modify any file.
